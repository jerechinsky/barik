# Third-Party Notices

## barik-but-better

The Codex usage widget's local-session snapshot selection, popup structure,
and Codex icon were adapted from
[`bottlebrushes/barik-but-better`](https://github.com/bottlebrushes/barik-but-better)
at commit `06b6bbff759eecfd0d38853746a43e666a5a779b`.

The drag-to-reorder behavior, hourly weather popup, event-driven calendar and
Wi-Fi refresh approach, and agenda event details were also adapted from that
repository. Their presentation and integration were
rewritten to preserve this fork's existing widgets, configuration, Celsius
weather, Focus Timer overlay, and custom active-space styling.
